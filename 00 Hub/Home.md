## The Story in Brief

A party of unlikely travellers converges on the town of [[Monsoth]] and is drawn westward toward [[Osgog]] — the Black Lake at the edge of the known world. Along the way they encounter the legacy of ancient gods ([[Torval]], [[Ceridwen]], [[Mons]]), face the reach of [[The God King]], and are guided — and tested — by the inscrutable [[The Boatman]].

The campaign spans [[The Cradle]] (the world's living heart), the ruins of old civilisations, sacred stone circles, and a waterfall that falls into the night sky.
