# The World — Cosmology & Mythology

The world of *[[The Black Lake of Osgog]]* is built on layers of myth, forgotten theology, and living landscape. Below is a working map of what is known.

---

## The Three Siblings

The foundational mythology centres on three siblings who overthrew Darkness:

| Name | Domain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Torval]] | Fire | One of the three; craftsman's patron |
| [[Ceridwen]] | Naming / Song | One of the three; language and magic |
| [[Mons]] | The Wheel | One of the three; fate and cycles |

Their sons — [[Osgog]] and [[Bremphyr]] ("One Eye"), twin artificers — forged weapons for the siblings.

## The God King & The Sun King

A force of domination operating through [[The Red Blade (NPC)]] and the [[Red Guard]]. [[Balthazar]] was experimented on by his agents.

- [[The God King]] — the entity; distant, commanding
- [[The Red Blade (NPC)]] — instrument of [[The God King]]'s will
- See [[The Red Guard and the Red Blade (Battle)]] for first encounter

Counterforce: [[Mother Night]] (referenced in [[Balthazar]]'s backstory; possibly [[Rhiannon]] or another aspect)
