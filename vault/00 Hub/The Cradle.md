# The Cradle

> *The living heart of the world — a place, a force, or both.*

The Cradle is one of the campaign's deepest mysteries. It is referenced in lore and may be a physical location, a cosmological state, or the name the world gives to its own living core.

---

## What Is Known

- The Cradle is associated with the living, breathing quality of the world in [[Nyth]]
- It relates to [[The Black Water]] and possibly to [[Osgog]] (the lake)
- The [[Ancient Tree]], [[Circles, Sacred Groves, and Wells]], and the [[The Trilithon]] may all be outcroppings of the same force
- The [[Bears]] may be its guardians, emissaries, or symptoms
- [[The Mornaswydh]] may be its shadow-side or its wound

---

## What Is Suspected

- That [[The Boatman]] knows what the Cradle is and will not say directly
- That [[The God King]]'s reach is weakened within it
- That the party's journey has been moving toward it since the beginning
