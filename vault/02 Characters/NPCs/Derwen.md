A type of tree found throughout our journey.

[[Mag]] gave us a derwen sapling that we planted over the grave of the nameless woman in [[Tawesek]].  It grew supernaturally fast, over night it was a mature tree.

Associated with [[The Abbot|Bryok]], [[Mother Night]], and more.

A symbol for one who gives and takes.
