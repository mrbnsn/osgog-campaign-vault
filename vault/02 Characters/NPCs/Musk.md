[[Tewlder]]’s brother, Musk is a ragged, semi homeless guy who lives in a three sided shelter close to the temple. Rumored to have caused the [[Tawesek#Summary of events in Tawesek|earthquakes]].

Was in the caves below [[Tawesek]] when the earthquakes started.  Escaped, found the child, and brought her through the caves.  Gave child to Gwen (of the Tower).
