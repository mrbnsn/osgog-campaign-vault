A ruffian we [[Battles#Ruffians on the Road to Tawesek|fight on the road]] to [[Tawesek]]. We end up taking him prisoner. He does most of the digging to get us out of the collapsed cave, and we force him to carry a trunk of treasure back to the monastery.
