A woman we met in the river-side village of [[Meander]].

> I am of the people of [[Doldhelan]] and [[Ceridwen]] - women of the forest and high crag.  The fen and the meadow.
>
> I remember far into the past, though my memories are clouded by the stories told about them - as is usual for us all.
>
> But I can listen to the earth, and the sky, and the waters.  And see the strength in them.She and [[Branwen]] spoke of many things.

Kelynnen guided us to the grove where [[Musk]] had been hiding out, with the centaurs.
