   1. The [[Sun King]]
Why would someone sent by the Sun King react negatively to [[Silas]] specifically?

Who is this guy?  Just a man, or a god?  What the hell does he want?

- Theory: [[Religions|The Sun King is Mons]].
   - Folks in Trenowydh mentioned that the Sun King would be returning soon.
   - [[Mons]] is supposed to return once the earth trembles and quakes - which it has.
   - Mons is a son of the "God King".  It may be that Sun King is a bastardization of Son King...Son of [[The God King]]?
   - The beast [[Osgog]] (Mons' son) released was likely a dragon.  The Sun King has ties to [[Runic Messages|dragons - Balthazar]], [[Mali]]...
   - Mons suffered a fatal [[Mons and the God of Death|wound in his battle with "the god of death"]].  Perhaps if he returns to this land while some remnant of that battle still remains, it could be fatal to Mons.
   - The Sun King is fixated on the [[The Black Water|Black Stream]], which is reported to be the blood of an ancient evil trapped under a mountain ([[The Abbot|Bryok]]).
   - The Sun King's minions have reacted badly to Silas - who is directly tied to the awakening of [[Religions|The Bear]], who would likely be opposed to Mons...
