Being clearly outnumbered and outmatched, [[Storr]] decides to challenge the [[The Red Blade (NPC)|Red Blade]] to one on one combat.  The Red Blade approaches the [[Fountain]] and cleanses himself in its waters.  He appears now stronger and more virile than he had following the initial fight.  [[Storr]] falls valiantly. The rest of the party attacks and they manage to bring down the mighty swordsman.  As he falls, the [[Red Guard]] advance, and [[Silas]], now human again, yells “Enough!” Then eyeing each other, the party, and the guardsmen await their next move…
