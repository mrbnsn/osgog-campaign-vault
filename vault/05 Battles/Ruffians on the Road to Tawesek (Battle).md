During the fight, [[Silas]] seems to shift into a bear’s form. The gang suffers heavy losses and one of the ruffians ([[NPCs#Broggy|Broggy]]) escapes through the woods. After tracking him down, we tie him to a tree and encourage him to give us information regarding his purpose. He agrees to lead us to their camp. There we discover several trunks containing assorted merchandise created at the [[Tawesek|monastery Tawesek]].
