After a shared dream about the [[Ancient Tree]] and a reading of [[Geshwa]], we find ourselves by a stream. Some prepare to hunt. [[Cricket]] checks the water level, and hears grinding stone. Suddenly a giant rises out of the water.

Through the course of the battle, the stone giant continually targets [[Silas]], who seems to make the giant especially furious.
