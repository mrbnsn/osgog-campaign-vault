- In the bark is a runic message, as if scraped in with some charcoal.

The decoded message:

Traitor, my aid weakens. 

His long eyes glimpse you.

More and more.

Listen to the words.

As the dead loose (lose?) their grip

The cracks widen.

I watch the best I can

Drinking from

The well.
