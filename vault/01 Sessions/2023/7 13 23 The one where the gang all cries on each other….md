[[Storr]] shares a story from her past of heart ache and rejection:

From Storr's past, there were twin siblings.  A brother and sister.  Storr was to be married to the brother as part of the next generation of leadership within the tribe - not a marriage of love but duty.  But, Storr loved the brother's sister.  The clan had no issues with same sex relationships but Storrs' lot in the tribe meant she had to bear children.  When the fateful battle occurred and she left the lands she never knew what became of any of them.

The brother, Bragnon, was a good, honorable man and warrior.  He cared for, and found her attractive (most do) but he also respected her.  Bragnon also knew that Storr loved his sister, so there's an unrequited attraction.  The twin sister, Norag however, she's straight and while she respects Storr does not reciprocate the feelings.  As Bragnon is respectful, Norag is selfish.  They love each other though fiercely (*not in a Game of Thrones Lannister way*).  Storr wasn’t sure what became of them or anyone else in the tribe and she left.
