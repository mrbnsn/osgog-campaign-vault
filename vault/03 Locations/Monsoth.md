Monsoth is the [[Maps|main settlement in our area]], and where we begin our quest.  It is sited on [[Foamflower|a peninsula with three hill]]<nowiki/>s, in the middle of which is a [[Circles, Sacred Groves, and Wells#Monsoth|sacred grove]].  It’s a maritime port.  Off the coast there is an island called “[[Foamflower|The Isle of Mons]]” which is rumored to be where the settlement started.

We meet refugees from Monsoth when we return to [[Tawesek|Kel/Tawesek]] after visiting [[Mysterious Places (and Some Less Mysterious)#Trenewydh|Trenewydh]].
