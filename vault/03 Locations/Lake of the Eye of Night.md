Also been referred to as "Vast Grohmlin." This is a perfectly circular lake in the far north of the continent. [[Silas]] and his family spied this lake from a lone mountain peak in the bay while seal hunting.

The lake also appeared in [[Silas#Tawesek|Silas' dream at Tawesek]].
