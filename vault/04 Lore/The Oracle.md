Found in the middle of the black lake, they gave us our 3 choices:

The party comes across an [[The Oracle|oracle]] within the rock island in the center of [[The Black Lake of Osgog]]. She [[11/30/23 The one where we follow the badger and meet the oracle#The Choices|gives them these choices before dismissing them]]:

1. Shall you continue this search and travel towards the mountains amongst the stars
1. Shall you find the one eyed man who waits by the grave of his mother
1. Or will you travel through darkness at the hand of [[The Boatman]] and give and receive your gifts.
